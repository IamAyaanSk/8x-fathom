Share is wired end-to-end: owners can copy a public link, and viewers get video, summary, transcript, and action items without signing in.

**How it works**
- When a meeting becomes `ready`, the worker assigns a `shareSlug`. Existing ready meetings get one on first **Share** click (`POST /api/v1/meetings/:id/share`).
- Public APIs (`GET /api/v1/share/:shareSlug` and `/transcript`) sit **outside** session auth. They only return data if the slug exists and processing is `ready`.
- `/share/$shareSlug` is a public page: player, summary, transcript, attendees, read-only action items. No Ask Fathom, scratchpad, or chat.

**Owner UX**
- Share is enabled when the call is ready. Click copies `https://…/share/{slug}`.

**Checked**
- Invalid slug: API `404`, page shows “This shared call is unavailable.”
- Sign in from that page reaches login.

Open a processed call and hit **Share**, then open the copied URL in a private window to confirm video + transcript seek. Meetings that were ready before this change get a slug the first time you share them.
